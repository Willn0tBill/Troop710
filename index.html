More about Scouting America
Where is Troop 710 located?

Troop 710 is listed in Hacienda Heights, California. Contact troop leadership through TroopWebHost to confirm the current meeting location and schedule.

Do I need outdoor experience to join?

Scouting is designed around learning new skills. You can ask troop leaders about what to bring to a visit and how beginners get started.

What happens at a troop meeting?

Scouts BSA troop meetings often involve planning activities, learning practical skills, teamwork, and youth leadership. Ask Troop 710 for its current meeting format and calendar.

How do I find the troop calendar or member resources?

Use the TroopWebHost directory to locate Troop 710 in Hacienda Heights. Some calendars, forms, or account information may only be available to registered members.

How can parents learn more about joining?

Start with the troop's TroopWebHost listing and connect with adult leadership. Scouting America's official site also explains program eligibility, safety standards, and family involvement.
