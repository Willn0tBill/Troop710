Troop 710 is a Scouting community serving Hacienda Heights. Our public resources include troop guidelines, youth leadership expectations, registration materials, and advancement documents.

  • Opportunities to build real-world skills
  • Leadership through participation and teamwork
  • Service guided by Scout values
