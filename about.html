    Welcome to Troop 710

    A community for growth, service, and adventure.

    Troop 710 is a Scouting America troop based in Hacienda Heights, California. Scouting gives young people opportunities to develop outdoor skills, take responsibility, contribute through service, and build confidence through teamwork. Our public website brings together useful information and points families toward the troop's official member resources.

    Looking for meeting details? Please use the official TroopWebHost portal for up-to-date locations, schedules, and contact information. These details are not repeated here unless verified.
    The Scouting experience

    Learn by doing.

    Scouting is about practical experiences and meaningful responsibility — growing skills that stay useful long after a meeting or campout.

    01 / EXPLORE

    Outdoor skills

    Build confidence outside, practice preparedness, and work with others to solve real-world challenges.

    02 / LEAD

    Youth leadership

    Develop communication, planning, and teamwork through the Scout patrol and leadership system.

    03 / SERVE

    Community impact

    Put the Scout values into action by identifying ways to help and making a lasting contribution.
